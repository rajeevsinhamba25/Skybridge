About the Business

What we do

We book cargo space on freighter and passenger-belly flights, handle pickup, airport screening, loading and final delivery, and give customers a live status for every air waybill (AWB).

Target customers

E-commerce sellers, pharma and medical suppliers, electronics importers, spare-parts distributors and any business with urgent, high-value freight.

Service area

Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata, with pickup and delivery in the surrounding city zones.

Value proposition

Next-day delivery on major routes, temperature-controlled options, transparent per-kg pricing and shipment tracking without needing to call anyone.

## Business problem

Shippers using air freight often have no visibility once cargo leaves their warehouse. They do not know whether a consignment is booked, airborne, held at screening or delivered, so they call the cargo desk repeatedly and lose time on delayed shipments.

## Solution

SkyBridge Air Cargo is a fictional domestic air freight company. This site gives customers self-service shipment tracking by AWB number and gives the cargo manager a one-screen operations dashboard.

## Customer

E-commerce sellers, pharma and medical distributors, electronics importers and spare-parts distributors who need next-day delivery between major Indian airports.

## Site sections

1. **Home** - business name, logo, purpose, "Get Started" button
2. **About** - what we do, target customers, service area, value proposition
3. **Services** - price table (Express, Standard, Cold Chain, Dangerous Goods, Bulk Charter)
4. **Dashboard** - 8 KPIs, a bar chart (shipments per month) and a donut chart (status mix)
5. **Contact / Enquiry** - email, phone, location, enquiry form

## Interactive business feature

**Track shipment.** Enter an AWB number in the Track section. Sample numbers: `AWB1001`, `AWB1002`, `AWB1003`, `AWB1004`.

## Hyperlinks and their business purpose

| Link | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Home "Get Started" to Track | Moves a visitor straight to the main feature |
| Services note to Contact | Turns a price query into an enquiry |
| Dashboard note to Track | Lets a manager drill into one consignment |
| Google Maps | Shows the cargo terminal location |
| Email (mailto) and Phone (tel) | Direct contact with the cargo desk |
| LinkedIn | Company updates and hiring |
| Airports Authority of India | Airport and cargo terminal information |
| BCAS | Air cargo security regulations |

## Dashboard: what the manager needs to know immediately

Total shipments, delivered, in transit, delayed, on-time delivery %, cargo handled (tonnes), average turnaround time and customer satisfaction. All figures are sample data.

1. Create a repository named `Abhishek-AirCargoApp` (or similar).

3. Go to Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > `main` / root > Save.
4. After a minute the site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
